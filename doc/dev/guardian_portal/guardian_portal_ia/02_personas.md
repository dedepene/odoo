# Guardian Portal Information Architecture

## Overview
The Guardian Portal serves as a centralized platform for guardians to manage their child's activities, view schedules, and communicate with coaches.

## User Roles
- **Guardian**: Primary role with access to view and manage their child's information.

## Main Navigation
- Home
- My Kids
- Sessions
- Billing
- Notifications
- Profile

## Key Features
### Viewing Child Schedules
Guardians can view upcoming sessions for their children, including details such as date, time, and session type.

### Reporting Absences
Guardians can proactively report absences for their children, ensuring coaches are informed in advance.

### Accessing Billing Information
Guardians can view billing statements and payment history related to their child's sessions.

### Communicating with Coaches
Guardians can send messages to coaches regarding their child's progress or any concerns.

## Information Flow
- Guardians log in to the portal and navigate to the "My Kids" section to view their children's schedules.
- Absence requests can be submitted directly from the session details page.
- Billing information is accessible from the main navigation, allowing guardians to review charges and payment history.
